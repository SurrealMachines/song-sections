allin1 (All-In-One Music Structure Analyzer), by Taejun Kim. MIT License.

What this extension uses: the allin1 network, exported to ONNX from allin1 1.1.0 (models/allin1_harmonix_fold*.onnx,
which hold no weights), and its post-processing, ported to TypeScript (src/structure/allin1/). The weights of the 8
"harmonix" folds are downloaded, with the user's consent, from the author's Hugging Face repository
taejunkim/allinone (revision 379e5fd010b3fdd0ee8381ff8cbcfa51d70b5c19; model card licence: MIT).
